## Solution: Manual Download of Real Lavender Images

### Step 1: Visit Image Sources
Use these direct links to find real lavender photos:

**Primary Source (Recommended):**
- **Unsplash**: https://unsplash.com/s/photos/lavender
- **Search Terms**: "lavender spring", "lavender summer", "lavender fall", "lavender winter"

**Alternative Sources:**
- **Pixabay**: https://pixabay.com/images/search/lavender/
- **Pexels**: https://pexels.com/search/lavender/

### Step 2: Download Images
Download **2 images for each season** (8 total):

1. **Spring Lavender** (2 images):
   - Search: "lavender spring new growth" or "young lavender plant"
   - Look for: Young plants, new growth, early flowering
   - Save as: `lavender__spring__01.jpg` and `lavender__spring__02.jpg`

2. **Summer Lavender** (2 images):
   - Search: "lavender summer bloom" or "lavender purple flowers"
   - Look for: Full bloom, purple flowers, peak flowering
   - Save as: `lavender__summer__01.jpg` and `lavender__summer__02.jpg`

3. **Fall Lavender** (2 images):
   - Search: "lavender fall dried" or "lavender seed heads"
   - Look for: Dried flowers, seed heads, autumn colors
   - Save as: `lavender__fall__01.jpg` and `lavender__fall__02.jpg`

4. **Winter Lavender** (2 images):
   - Search: "lavender winter dormant" or "lavender gray foliage"
   - Look for: Dormant plants, gray foliage, winter care
   - Save as: `lavender__winter__01.jpg` and `lavender__winter__02.jpg`

### Step 3: Image Requirements
- **Size**: 800x600 pixels (or similar aspect ratio)
- **Format**: JPG
- **Quality**: High resolution, clear, well-lit
- **Content**: Actual photos of lavender plants
- **Seasonal Accuracy**: Images should represent the actual season

### Step 4: File Organization
Place all images in the `images/lavender/` directory with these exact names:
