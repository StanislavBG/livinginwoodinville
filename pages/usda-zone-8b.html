Seasonal Planting Guide & Tips

Spring (March - May)

Planting Schedule

  • March: Start cool-season vegetables indoors (lettuce, spinach, broccoli)
  • April: Plant cool-season crops outdoors after last frost
  • May: Transplant warm-season vegetables (tomatoes, peppers)

Garden Tasks

  • Prune dormant trees and shrubs
  • Prepare soil with compost
  • Start warm-season seeds indoors

Summer (June - August)

Planting Schedule

  • June: Plant heat-loving vegetables (okra, sweet potatoes)
  • July: Succession plant quick crops (lettuce, radishes)
  • August: Start fall crops for winter harvest

Garden Tasks

  • Water deeply and regularly
  • Harvest early crops
  • Mulch to retain moisture

Fall (September - November)

Planting Schedule

  • September: Plant trees and shrubs
  • October: Plant spring bulbs and garlic
  • November: Plant cover crops

Garden Tasks

  • Harvest late-season crops
  • Clean up garden beds
  • Prepare for winter protection

Winter (December - February)

Planning & Maintenance

  • December: Plan next year's garden layout
  • January: Order seeds and plants
  • February: Start seeds indoors for spring

Garden Tasks

  • Protect tender plants from frost
  • Prune dormant trees
  • Maintain garden tools

Essential Zone 8b Tips

Temperature Management

Protect tender plants during occasional cold snaps with frost cloth. Zone 8b can have unexpected freezes.

Watering Strategy

Water deeply but infrequently to encourage deep root growth. Mulch around plants to retain moisture.

Soil Preparation

Add compost annually and consider raised beds for better drainage. Zone 8b soils benefit from organic matter.

Pest Management

Mild winters mean some pests overwinter. Practice good garden hygiene and consider integrated pest management.
