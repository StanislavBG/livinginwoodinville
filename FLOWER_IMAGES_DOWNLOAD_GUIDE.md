## 📋 QUICK DOWNLOAD INSTRUCTIONS

### Step 1: Visit Unsplash.com
1. Go to [https://unsplash.com](https://unsplash.com)
2. Use the search terms provided for each image
3. Look for high-quality, garden-focused images

### Step 2: Download Images
1. Click on the image you want
2. Click the download button (free, no account required)
3. Save with the exact filename specified

### Step 3: Place in Correct Directories
- **Roses**: Place in `images/roses/`
- **Lavender**: Place in `images/lavender/`
- **Echinacea**: Place in `images/echinacea/`
- **Black-Eyed Susan**: Place in `images/black-eyed-susan/`
- **Salvia**: Place in `images/salvia/`

### Step 4: Test Your Site
1. Start your local server: `python3 server.py`
2. Visit: `http://localhost:3000/roses`
3. Check that images load correctly
